My Assessment; My Practice.

Production notes
Product captures come from the local Medsy application. Demo records were added only
to an isolated browser profile. No application source or normal user data was changed.
Moving cards, typing, highlights and workflow stages are illustrative motion graphics
composited over product captures, not a continuous recording of user interactions.
Question examples and product figures are demonstration content.
No November launch claim is made for the overall product.
